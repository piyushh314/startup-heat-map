      About India Startup Atlas

      A map of where India's startups are registered, built to compare places fairly. It shows official state counts, startups per lakh people, and 700+ districts from the Startup India register.

      Using it

      Zoom in to see heat turn into clusters and glowing district dots. Click a state bubble or a dot for details. Switch to per lakh people so large states do not always win. Turn on Gap finder to mark under-represented states. Use Compare, Top 10, search, and the PNG/CSV export.

      Data

      The source file has counts by place only. It has no sector, year or startup names, so those features are not included, and nothing is estimated to fill the gap.
